The challenge

Managing works should not manage you

Between tenants, owners, contractors and council, property works can quickly eat into your time and your margins. Unclear scopes lead to variations, missed consents create liability and poor workmanship brings tenants back on the phone.

studioBAM becomes your single, accountable point of contact. We scope it, document it, get it consented when needed and observe the work on site until it is signed off.

  • Clear, priced scopes of work
  • Fewer variations
  • Consent risk managed
  • Owner ready reporting
